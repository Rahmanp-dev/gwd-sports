🔔 Smart Fee Reminders

T-5 days: Friendly heads-up · Due date: Fee is due today · T+1 to T+3: Overdue · After T+3: Stops automatically. No harassment. Capped at 3/day.

One tap records cash payment, immediately cancels pending fee reminders, and marks the fee as settled. Clean books.

15
Getting Started

Your First 10 Minutes

GWD will provide you with admin login credentials. Here's what to do first:

1

Log In to Your Admin Dashboard

Visit your academy URL and log in with the credentials provided by GWD.

2

Complete the Setup Checklist

The dashboard shows a proactive checklist of what needs to be configured. Follow it top to bottom.

3

Set Your Fee Schedule

Fees tab → Create a fee plan. Choose Monthly / Quarterly / Half-Yearly / Annual and set the amount. Without this, parents cannot make payments.

4

Create Your First Batch

Check-in tab → Add Batch. Name it (e.g., "Morning 6–8 AM"), assign a time slot and days. This generates the QR code.

5

Upload Your Logo & Set Brand Colors

Branding tab → Upload your logo, write your tagline, and pick your colors. Immediately applies to website, passports, and receipts.

💡

Pro Tip

Once fees, batch, and branding are set, your academy website is live. Share the URL with parents immediately.
