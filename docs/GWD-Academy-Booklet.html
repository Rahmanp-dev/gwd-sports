The idea

It isn't a toolkit.
It's a loop.

Most software hands you eight separate tools and leaves the joining-up to you. GWD is built the other way round: each stage produces exactly what the next one needs, and the last stage feeds the first. Run it once and it starts turning on its own.

Attendance Live

Present should mean
actually present.

Mode one

The parent scans

Print the batch QR and put it at the entrance. A parent scans as their child arrives, the child is marked present, and the parent has a WhatsApp confirmation before they've reached the car.

Mode two

The coach marks

The coach opens the trainer portal, sees the batch roster and taps down the list. Every parent is notified at once. No phone needed at the gate.

Admin → Check-in
Batches, check-in windows and printable QR codes.

📍 Location-verified

Set a radius around your ground and a scan only counts inside it. A QR code photographed and sent home stops working.

⏰ Session windows

Each batch has its hours. Scans outside them are refused, so yesterday's code can't mark today's register.

🔀 Merged cleanly

If the parent scans and the coach also marks, it's one attendance and one message — never a duplicate to the parent.

The message that earns the renewal: “Rohan checked in at MasterGrade Cricket Academy at 5:32 PM ✅”. A parent at work, who has no other way of knowing their child arrived safely, gets that every session. No brochure you print will ever be worth as much.

Part Five

What we promise you,
in plain words.

Not terms and conditions. These are the six decisions the platform is actually built around, written the way we'd say them to you across a table.

🔒

Your academy's data is sealed off from every other academy

Isolation is enforced at the database layer, not by hiding a menu. You cannot see another academy's students, revenue or messages, and nobody can see yours.

🧒

We never punish a child for a billing problem

Attendance, passports and portal access stay open regardless of what's owed. An unpaid fee is a conversation between adults.

📏

Your website will never claim something that isn't true

If you have forty students it says forty. The platform cannot generate a "500+ athletes trained" badge and has no facility for inventing testimonials — for you or for anyone you're competing with on the map.

💸

We never take a cut of your coaching fee

Our revenue is a 1% platform fee, added to the parent's payment alongside the payment gateway's own charge — not deducted from yours. Your fee settles to your bank in full, automatically, and we never hold it in between. The full split is on page 06.

🚪

You can leave whenever you want, with your data

No lock-in, no notice period, no exit fee. Ask and we'll export your students, attendance and payment history for you.

🗣️

We tell you what isn't built yet

Everything in Part Three is marked In build because it is. You will not find a feature in this guide that we've implied you can use today when you can't.

Why write these down at all? Because an academy handing over its student records, its parent relationships and its fee collection to a platform is taking a real risk. The least we can do is be specific in advance about how we intend to behave — and then be held to it.
