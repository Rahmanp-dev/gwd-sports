GWD Platform — Edge Cases & Adoption Architecture

Payment flow edge cases, bulk onboarding mechanics, parent retention design, and the communication engine that ties it all together.

⚠ Resolve before scaling the payment flow further
Route auto-split may not be lawful for most of your academies yet

The flow in your screenshot — parent pays ₹3,075, Razorpay Route auto-splits ₹3,000 to the academy and ₹75 to GWD/Razorpay — relies on automatic split-settlement. Per RBI's Payment Aggregator Directions (Sept 2025), this is only permitted for merchants with domestic annual turnover above ₹40 lakh. Most 40–80 student academies (₹12–38L/year) sit under that line. Confirm with Razorpay or payments counsel whether your founding cohort needs a different settlement mechanism before this scales past pilot volume.

Critical — resolve first
Refund and cancellation clawback Critical

Parent needs a refund after academy (₹3,000) and GWD (₹13.50) have already been settled. Your margin per transaction is razor-thin — one unhandled refund can wipe out the margin from 5+ other transactions.

Fix: Razorpay Route supports linked refunds that automatically reverse the split proportionally. Confirm this is configured — don't build a manual clawback process as your primary path.
Academy crosses the ₹40L threshold mid-year Critical

As academies grow, some will cross the merchant turnover line during the year — the eligible settlement mechanism can change underneath a live account.

Fix: Build an internal quarterly turnover check per academy. Flag any academy approaching ₹35L for reclassification before they cross, not after.
High priority
Failed UPI AutoPay / mandate retry High

Insufficient balance, bank server downtime, or mandate expiry are common in India — not rare edge cases. A failed auto-debit with no retry logic silently breaks the monthly cycle.

Fix: Auto-retry on Day+1 and Day+3. If both fail, fall back to a manual payment link sent via WhatsApp rather than waiting for the next billing cycle.
Off-platform cash leakage High

An academy owner under pressure from a price-sensitive parent may quietly accept cash instead of routing through GWD, bypassing your fee entirely.

Fix: Tie functional benefits — passport updates, digital receipt, attendance QR unlock — to platform payment specifically, so paying off-platform visibly degrades the parent's own experience, not just GWD's revenue.
Fee amount changes mid-cycle High

Academy raises fees or a student moves to a higher batch. UPI AutoPay mandates are fixed-amount — the existing mandate doesn't auto-adjust.

Fix: Any fee change triggers automatic mandate cancellation + a re-authorization request sent immediately via WhatsApp, with a clear one-line explanation of what changed.
Medium priority
Multiple children, one parent Medium

Same parent phone/UPI paying for two children — possibly at different academies. Reconciliation must stay per-student, not per-parent.

Fix: Every payment link and mandate is scoped to a single Passport ID, never a parent account. A parent can hold multiple links, but each is independently trackable.
Multi-sport / multi-batch fee bundling Medium

A student in both cricket and swimming at one academy may have two fee lines. Bundled vs. separate transactions changes how your 2.5% and placement fee calculations apply.

Fix: Default to one consolidated monthly charge per student per academy, itemized internally, so your commission math stays simple and auditable.
Rounding on odd fee amounts Medium

2.5% of ₹2,750 or ₹3,333 produces non-round numbers, inviting "why is it ₹3,068.75" support queries.

Fix: Round the final parent-facing total up to the nearest rupee, disclosed as policy in the payment page footer.
Lower priority — design for later
Chargebacks / bank-initiated disputes Low, but plan ahead

Rare early on, but GWD sits as the facilitator between parent and academy — a real dispute-response workflow will eventually be needed.

Fix: Razorpay handles first-line dispute intake; document an internal escalation owner (likely your co-founder) before volume makes this urgent.
The bulk import flow — from register to activated parent
1
Owner onboarding session (White Glove)
A GWD team member sits with the owner in person or on a call. This is not self-serve for the first cohort — the friction of "figure it out yourself" is exactly what kills adoption at this stage.
2
Bulk student import — pick the fastest path for that owner
Register photo → OCR extraction, WhatsApp-forwarded list → auto-parsed, or CSV upload for tech-comfortable owners. All 40–100 students in within the hour, not spread across days.
3
Instant automated welcome burst — this is the "put into effect" moment
The moment a student record with a parent number is created, an automated WhatsApp fires immediately — not manually sent by the owner one by one. Owner should never be the bottleneck for activating 50 parents.
4
Welcome message does three jobs in one send
Introduces GWD in one line, shows the child's new Passport link, and includes the first payment link if a fee is currently due — combining onboarding and first monetization in a single touchpoint.
5
Owner gets an activation dashboard, not a blind database
A simple view: how many of 50 parents have clicked their link vs. still dormant. The owner personally chases stragglers — automation gets you 80%, the owner's relationship gets the last 20%.
Keep the minimum viable student record to name, phone, sport only on Day 1. Everything else — DOB, batch, fee amount, photo — gets filled progressively. A long form at import time is where bulk onboarding quietly dies.
Making it feel reliable — daily and weekly touchpoints
QR check-in → instant parent notification

Every coach scan triggers an automatic WhatsApp: "Rohan checked in at 5:00 PM ✅". This is your highest-frequency touchpoint — daily — and it taps a real emotional need: parents want to know their child arrived safely, not just that attendance was logged for admin purposes.

Weekly progress digest

Every Sunday evening: attendance %, any achievement, upcoming fee due date, one link to the full passport. Reinforces value even in weeks with no major event — this is what prevents the app from feeling forgotten between milestones.

Payment convenience as a behavioral lock-in

Once a parent has set up UPI AutoPay once, switching back to manual cash payment now has higher friction than staying. Default behavior is sticky — lean into "set it once, never think about it again" in your messaging.

Making it feel luxury — perception design
Visual polish on the passport page

The public passport and profile pages must look premium — not a generic admin dashboard. This is the single page most likely to be screenshotted and shared.

Proactive, not reactive, communication

"Rohan's monthly report is ready" arriving unprompted feels like a service. A parent having to log in and dig for it feels like a chore.

Exclusivity signaling

"Founding Academy," "GWD Verified" badges make a parent feel their child is part of something selective, not just a row in a database.

Shareable achievement cards

Auto-generated cards for milestones, sent straight to WhatsApp Status/Instagram. This is distribution and retention in the same motion — sharing publicly commits the parent to caring about the platform.

Reliability is earned in the small failures, not the big features. A payment receipt that's ever wrong, or an "absent" mark when the child was actually present, destroys trust faster than any feature can rebuild it. Build a coach-side same-day correction flow for attendance errors — this is a small technical feature with an outsized trust impact.
Channel hierarchy

WhatsApp Primary channel — ~98%+ open rates in India. Already have Interakt BSP integration. All time-sensitive and personal messages go here first.

SMS Fallback only — for WhatsApp delivery failures or numbers not on WhatsApp.

Email Tertiary — formal receipts and records, especially given GST/TCS reporting obligations. Most parents won't check it regularly, but it matters for paper trail.

Dashboard Owner-facing only — activation status, overdue flags, never sent directly to parents as a "channel."

Universal payment link — one link, reused every month

Every student gets one stable link: gwdpay.in/[passport_id]. It always reflects the current cycle's amount due, so parents bookmark it once instead of hunting for "this month's link" in chat history. After payment, the same link flips to a receipt/status view — "Paid ✅ Next due: [date]."

Frequency capping — don't let this become the reason they mute you

A parent getting attendance + payment reminder + achievement + broadcast + survey in one day will mute the number — and once muted, every future message (including the ones that matter) goes unseen.

Fix: Set a daily message budget per parent with priority order — payment due > attendance > achievement > general broadcast. Lower-priority messages get delayed or batched rather than sent regardless.
